Pushover: read the mechanism before the point

Linear or nonlinear: let the question decide

Material response, geometry and contact can change the solution. A bilinear example shows when elastic extrapolation stops being valid.

Industrial steel: design the complete load path

Mining structures require strength, stability, serviceability and equipment interfaces to work together; selecting member sizes is only one part of the process.

Abaqus: a fine mesh does not validate the model

A practical framework for checking what a finite-element model represents and what convergence actually establishes.

Seismic design: connect configuration, demand and detailing
