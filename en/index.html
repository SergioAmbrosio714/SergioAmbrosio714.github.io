Ten applications: problem, procedure and output
01

ETABS and SAP2000 results

Exported tables can differ between models, load cases and software versions.

Define the exchange format, identify cases and units, and retain the link to the source model.

Normalized review tables. The demonstration uses CSV; it does not connect to the CSI API.

02

Extraction, cleaning and normalization

A missing value or mixed unit can distort a comparison.

Validate headers, finite values, unique identifiers and physical dimensions before converting to SI.

A consistent dataset or an explicit error that prevents invalid data from being processed.

03

Internal forces and displacements

An isolated value hides where the maximum occurs and under which case.

Organize results by member, location and case; retain the sign convention and physical quantity.

Envelopes, profiles and critical points traceable to the original data.

04

Scenario comparison

Comparing models without controlling geometry and units can produce misleading conclusions.

Align levels and cases, document the reference scenario and distinguish absolute from relative differences.

Interpretable differences between scenarios; the storey demonstration includes a selectable reference case.

05

Reproducible spreadsheets

Manual editing makes inputs, formulas and revisions harder to trace.

Separate data from calculations, define output formats and check units, totals and required fields.

Processed CSV that can be imported into a spreadsheet, with explicit headers and units.

06

Technical plots and reporting

A plot loses meaning when it omits cases, units and scope.

Generate plots from the same exportable data and accompany them with parameters, conventions and limitations.

Downloadable SVG plots and CSV/JSON outputs from the demonstrations.

07

Nonlinear analysis with OpenSeesPy

Inelastic response depends on constitutive models, loading protocols and convergence.

Document materials, discretization and solution controls; process forces, deformations and convergence records.

Forces, deformations and records organised for model interpretation. The CFRP case identifies the reported findings and the files needed to reproduce them.

08

Parametric studies

Changing several parameters at once makes the response harder to explain.

Control scenarios, vary identifiable parameters and compare trends against an independent solution.

Comparison of stiffness, deformation and stress. The cantilever explores the cubic influence of section depth on second moment of area.

09

Units and consistency

Numerical convergence does not prove that inputs represent the intended problem.

Check dimensions, physical ranges, equilibrium, boundary conditions and limiting cases before interpreting results.

Automated tests and explicit checks, without fictitious code-compliance indicators.

10

AutoLISP and CAD documentation

Repetitive detailing requires consistency between geometry, reinforcement, dimensions and layers.

Parameterize beams, columns, walls and stairs; control longitudinal bars, ties and drawing conventions.

Details organised for review. Source code and screenshots of the described routines have yet to be published.

06 / ABOUT

Sergio Junior Ambrosio Camayo

I am a Civil Engineer with a professional degree from Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería. I design and review steel and concrete structures for buildings, mining and infrastructure. I currently work on structural engineering for mining projects at SRK.

I approach a project by understanding its structural system, support conditions and load paths. I connect code requirements with modelling assumptions, member checks and the documentation needed to review each decision.

Seismic and nonlinear analysis are part of my specialisation. I use pushover procedures and tools such as Abaqus to study structural behaviour, with close attention to materials, boundary conditions and the limits of each idealisation. Python helps me organise results, compare scenarios and repeat calculations with explicit units.

In technical review and design optimisation, I focus on consistency between the analysis, connections, foundations and drawings. For me, professional responsibility includes explaining assumptions, acknowledging uncertainty and providing a sound basis for design decisions.
