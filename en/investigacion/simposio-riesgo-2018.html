Seismic performance of base-isolated water storage tanks

Numerical comparison of fixed-base concrete tanks and tanks isolated with triple pendulum bearings.

Method

Equivalent mechanical models and nonlinear dynamic analysis using seven pairs of bidirectional records. Response measures include shear, overturning moment, sloshing and base displacement.

Parameters investigated

SystemVariables
Tank–waterH/R; t/R; constant radius of 10 m
IsolationEffective periods of 3 and 4 s; damping of 10% and 25%
DemandSLE, DBE and MCE

Findings and limits

The investigated cases show reduced shear and moment alongside displacement demand at the base. Sloshing varies little between models.
