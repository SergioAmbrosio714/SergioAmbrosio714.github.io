A study of seismic isolation for reinforced concrete liquid storage tanks using triple pendulum bearings.

Method and parameters

Time-history analysis of 45 cases using 14 pairs of records. Variables: H/R, effective period, damping and SLE/DBE/MCE seismic hazard levels.

Response measures

  • Base shear and overturning moment.
  • Sloshing and base displacement.

Findings reported in the abstract

The abstract reports demand mitigation with triple pendulum bearings and sensitivity to H/R and periods of 3 and 4 s. Findings apply to the investigated parameter set.

One paper, two documentary references

The proceedings title is retained in the bibliographic reference. The programme uses the alternative title ‘SEISMIC RESPONSE OF BASE-ISOLATED REINFORCED CONCRETE LIQUID STORAGE TANKS USING TRIPLE PENDULUM SYSTEMS’. Both entries share C000391 and refer to one paper.

2c-0305 identifies the proceedings document; 2g-0296 is the presentation identifier in the SOP programme. Sergio Junior Ambrosio Camayo is listed as the speaker.
