Project overview

CEPITOCE involves steel structures for high-voltage electrical infrastructure: towers, gantries and equipment supports. I contributed to their design and verification at HMV Ingenieros in 2026.

The work connects each structure’s function with its strength, stability and load transfer. Assessment of design alternatives and preparation of technical documentation were part of the scope.

My contribution

As a Structural Engineer — Professional III at HMV, I contributed to the design of high-voltage towers and gantries and the verification of steel supports for electrical equipment.

  • Design and review of steel structural members.
  • Assessment and optimization of strong, lightweight and efficient solutions.
  • Development of design criteria, calculation reports and technical deliverables.
  • Technical coordination and guidance for an engineering intern.

Towers, gantries and equipment supports

These three structural types require different considerations. For towers and gantries, geometry and support conditions govern the overall response. For equipment supports, the equipment interface determines how actions enter the structure.

In each case, the load path must continue through members, connections and supports. Modelled stiffness needs to be consistent with the details that transfer these actions. These principles explain the review approach without assuming a specific CEPITOCE configuration.

Verification and optimization

Design optimization was part of the work. It involves comparing alternatives against consistent criteria, actions and support conditions, so that a lighter solution retains the required performance.

Reviewing a steel structure connects overall behaviour with component checks. Stability and second-order effects are assessed according to the structural system and the adopted analysis procedure.

  • Relate global demands to member checks.
  • Maintain consistency between modelled stiffness and force transfer through connections.
  • Retain compatible load cases when reviewing reactions and supports.
  • Carry design changes through to calculations and drawings.

Foundations and interfaces

My work at HMV also covered foundations for towers, gantries and equipment, together with review of models, calculation reports and drawings. The available project-specific description of CEPITOCE focuses on the steel structures.

For these structural types, the structure–foundation interface requires consistent axes, signs, units and concurrent actions. Reviewing supports and reactions together connects the model response with structural and geotechnical foundation criteria.
