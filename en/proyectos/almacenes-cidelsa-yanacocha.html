Project overview

ALMACENES CIDELSA / YANACOCHA is a steel multipurpose tent structure with overall dimensions of approximately 20 × 30 m. My contribution in 2024 covered structural modelling, design and verification.

The scope connects overall analysis with primary members, connections and foundations. The calculation report and Revit drawings communicate that relationship, in coordination with architecture and MEP.

My contribution

My work covered steel structural design, member and connection checks, foundation design and multidisciplinary coordination.

  • Modelling and analysis of the steel structure in SAP2000.
  • Design and verification of primary members and connections.
  • Foundation design and review of transferred actions.
  • Calculation reports, Revit drawings and coordination with architecture and MEP.

Structural system and load path

The steel structure supports a multipurpose tent facility. Its overall dimensions establish the scale of the work but do not by themselves define height, frame spacing, bracing or support conditions.

Understanding the system connects the global model with the members receiving and transferring actions. The detailed structural arrangement belongs to the project models and drawings, which are not published here.

From the global model to connections

SAP2000, Mathcad and IDEA StatiCa connect overall analysis with member and connection checks. Moving between these stages requires forces from compatible cases and consistent stiffness and support assumptions.

A connection must be considered as part of the system: its force transfer and deformation capacity need to be consistent with the behaviour represented in the global model. Reviewing the detail completes the interpretation of analysis demands.

  • Global model: geometry, actions, supports and stability.
  • Members: demands and checks for the primary structural members.
  • Connections: transferred forces and consistency with model assumptions.
  • Documentation: agreement between calculations, details and drawings.

Foundations and coordination

Foundation studies in SAFE complete the load path from the superstructure into its supports. Reactions must retain their axes, signs and load cases so that foundation checks remain consistent with the global analysis.

Coordination with architecture and MEP brings structural geometry together with functional requirements. Revit drawings document the coordinated solution alongside the calculation report.
