APPLIED JUDGEMENT / CONCEPTUAL EXAMPLES

From the code to engineering reasoning.

A

A · Reinforced concrete building

Connect actions with global response, member design and foundations through a single design basis.

Conceptual workflow · no results from a built project

  1. 01 · Establish the design basis

    Identify site, occupancy, submission status, structural system and applicable editions. For a Peruvian submission, resolve the E.030 transition before generating the spectrum; an ACI reference does not automatically replace RNE.

    RNE E.030RNE E.060ACI 318

  2. 02 · Build the load register

    Separate self-weight, finishes, partitions, occupancy and equipment. Record tributary areas, seismic mass, combinations and units, avoiding duplicate loads.

    RNE E.020RNE E.030

  3. 03 · Represent the system

    Define levels, diaphragms, effective stiffness, restraints and continuity into the foundations. Record assumptions affecting periods, force distribution and displacements.

    RNE E.030RNE E.060

  4. 04 · Review the analysis

    Check mass and equilibrium; examine modes, torsion, shear distribution and drift. Results need an identified case, combination and response state to be meaningful.

    RNE E.030

  5. 05 · Design members and foundations

    Connect compatible demands with flexural, shear, interaction and detailing checks. Coordinate reactions and combinations with the geotechnical report, distinguishing structural strength, bearing pressures and settlement.

    RNE E.060RNE E.050ACI 318

  6. 06 · Document the design decisions

    Link each check to its input, criterion, edition, result and drawing. Python can normalize tables and flag inconsistencies; selection of criteria and engineering review remain the engineer's responsibility.

    RNE E.020RNE E.030RNE E.050RNE E.060
Reviewable deliverable

Load register, reviewed model, verification matrix, calculation report and coordinated drawings.

Educational workflow for a generic building. It does not describe SUNSETGOLF results or establish that its checks used the editions consulted here.

B

B · Industrial steel structure

Follow actions from equipment and framing through connections, anchors and foundations.

Conceptual workflow · no results from a built project

  1. 01 · Define operation and the code basis

    Distinguish operation, maintenance, erection and exceptional conditions. Identify jurisdictional and contractual standards and choose a coherent framework for loads and resistance.

    RNE E.020ASCE/SEI 7AISC 360

  2. 02 · Control equipment interfaces

    Obtain manufacturer loads, axes, application points, concurrent states, frequencies and limits. Do not add maxima from incompatible states or present estimated loads as final information.

    RNE E.020ASCE/SEI 7

  3. 03 · Review stability and the model

    Represent bracing, supports and connection stiffness. Evaluate second-order effects and buckling lengths or conditions using the adopted method, including relevant erection stages.

    AISC 360

  4. 04 · Check members and connections

    Assess limit states and interaction without mixing LRFD and ASD. Follow forces through plates, bolts and welds; consider AISC 341 when required by the seismic system and design basis.

    AISC 360AISC 341AWS D1.1/D1.1M

  5. 05 · Review serviceability and foundations

    Compare deflections and vibration with functional and equipment requirements. Review compatible reactions, uplift, anchors, pedestals and ground using the relevant structural and geotechnical criteria.

    AISC 360RNE E.050RNE E.060

  6. 06 · Document decisions

    Retain criteria, assumptions, alternatives and connection reviews. Python can compare scenarios and organize reactions; equipment limits, stability and detailing require engineering interpretation.

    AISC 360ASCE/SEI 7
Reviewable deliverable

Design basis, operating-state matrix, model, member and connection checks, and foundation interfaces.

A generic industrial structure. Use of AISC 341 or AWS D1.1 is not attributed to CEPITOCE, CIDELSA or SRK. Personal application of E.090 remains unconfirmed.

C

C · Assessment and retrofit

Connect evidence, diagnosis, performance objectives and the intervention mechanism before choosing a strengthening material.

Conceptual workflow · no results from an actual intervention

  1. 01 · Establish the existing condition

    Distinguish documented, observed, tested and inferred information. Record geometry, materials, damage, changes in use and data reliability before defining capacities.

    ACI 562ASCE/SEI 41

  2. 02 · Define the objective and assessment framework

    Specify the deficiency, hazard and target performance. Select procedures and acceptance criteria for existing structures rather than automatically transferring new-building requirements.

    ASCE/SEI 41ACI 562

  3. 03 · Formulate and check the model

    Represent relevant mechanisms, stiffness, strength and supports. Distinguish numerical convergence, sensitivity and validation; property uncertainty must remain explicit.

    ASCE/SEI 41

  4. 04 · Compare demand and capacity

    Review actions and deformations consistent with the procedure. Identify brittle mechanisms, connections and governing components before interpreting global capacity.

    ASCE/SEI 41ACI 562

  5. 05 · Select the intervention

    Compare jacketing, FRP and other alternatives against the mechanism. For FRP, assess substrate, orientation, bond, anchorage and the chosen document's seismic scope; CODE-440.13 does not cover every application addressed by the guide.

    ACI 562ACI 440.2R / ACI PRC-440.2

  6. 06 · Review the modified system

    Reassess redistribution, compatibility, foundations and construction stages. Document uncertainty, inspection and acceptance criteria; a local strength increase alone does not establish a global improvement.

    ASCE/SEI 41ACI 562RNE E.050
Reviewable deliverable

Evidence-based diagnosis, assessment objective, model with explicit uncertainty, alternatives comparison and an intervention verification programme.

Conceptual method. It does not reproduce tests, validate the reported CFRP case results or constitute an executable intervention for an existing structure.
